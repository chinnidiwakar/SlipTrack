# SlipTrack 🛡️
**Mindful Recovery & Urge Tracking for Android**

SlipTrack is a privacy-first, offline-only tool designed to help users navigate recovery through mindfulness and discipline. Unlike traditional trackers, SlipTrack uses the Hindu philosophy of the **Three Gunas** (Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas) to visually represent progress through a dynamic, streak-based sky.

---

## ✨ Features

* **The Sky:** A dynamic background that shifts based on your streak:
* **Emergency Tools:** Dual-mode SOS screen featuring an **Urge Wave** visualizer and **Box Breathing** guide to help navigate difficult moments.
* **Privacy by Design:** Zero cloud sync. Zero data collection. All data lives in a local Room database on your device.
* **Future-Proof Tech:** Built entirely with **Jetpack Compose**, Kotlin Coroutines, and Flow for a reactive, modern UI.
* **Backup & Restore:** Export your data to JSON to keep your history safe when switching devices.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
* **UI Framework:** [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose)
* **Local Database:** [Room](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room)
* **Data Storage:** [Jetpack DataStore](https://developer.android.com/topic/libraries/architecture/datastore) (Preferences)
* **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)

## 📜 Philosophy
The app is built on the principle that discipline is choosing between what you want *now* and what you want *most*. By visualizing the "mental weather" through the Gunas, users can observe their urges without acting on them—treating them as waves to be ridden rather than enemies to be fought.

## 🛡️ Privacy
SlipTrack does not require an internet connection. We believe your recovery journey is yours alone. 
* **No Analytics**
* **No Trackers**
* **No Cloud Servers**
